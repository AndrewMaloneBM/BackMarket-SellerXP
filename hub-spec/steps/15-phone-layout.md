# Step 15: Phone layout

## Goal
The hub home works on a phone.

## Read
Picture: `screens/07-phone.png`. Source: `source/07-phone.html`.

## Do
Under 720px wide:
1. Header: the Back Market symbol (32px) instead of the wordmark, then the team name. On the right: the "How to set up" link, then "Add a prototype" as a black 48px icon button with a plus.
2. Title "Prototype Hub" at 42px.
3. The "What's new" link moves to its own line under the title.
4. The search input is full width.
5. The filter chips sit on one line that scrolls sideways. They do not wrap.
6. No cards or list toggle. Cards only, in one column.
7. Every control you tap is at least 48px high.
8. Above the footer, show an info block: "Prototypes are built for a laptop screen. You can read the cards here and open them later on desktop."

## Leave out
A phone version of the list view.

## Checklist
- At 390px wide nothing is cut off and the page does not scroll sideways.
- Only the chip row scrolls sideways.
- Chips and buttons are at least 48px high.
- The info block shows on phones only.
- At 720px and wider, the desktop layout from the earlier steps is unchanged.
